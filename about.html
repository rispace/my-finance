Your money, at a glance.
Private by design.

Finance is a free, open-source app for tracking spending, earnings, budgets, credit cards, loans and what you own. There’s no account to create and no company server: your data lives on your device and, if you choose, in a private folder in your own Google Drive so your phone and computer stay in sync.

Open Finance How to install

Everything in one place

Spending

Categories and subcategories, a monthly donut chart, 12-month trends, search, filters and CSV export.

Earnings

Paychecks, side income and interest; monthly totals, savings rate, and earned-vs-spent over a year.

Budgets

An overall limit plus lines you name yourself, with a pace marker that shows if you’re ahead of plan.

Credit cards

Balances, limits, utilization, payments and refunds, limit-change history, monthly summaries.

Loans

Car, mortgage, student or personal loans with an interest estimate for each payment.

Assets & net worth

Your car, home and savings with value history and equity, plus stocks and ETFs at live prices.

Install it (about 30 seconds)

Finance is a web app you add to your home screen. It then opens full-screen like any other app and works offline. Nothing to download from an app store.

First, open the link in your phone’s real browser. If you tapped the link inside Facebook, Messenger or another app, it opened in that app’s built-in browser, which can’t install apps. Tap ⋯ (or ⋮) in that window and choose Open in Safari / Open in browser, or copy rispace.github.io/my-finance into Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).

iPhone & iPad · Safari

Don’t see ⋯ or the address bar? Scroll up a little or tap the very bottom of the screen; Safari hides its toolbar while you scroll. If the page looks like a plain article with big text, Reader is on: tap the Reader icon in the address bar (or ⋯ → Hide Reader) to turn it off first.
See a Share button (a square with an arrow ↑) in the toolbar instead? That’s an older iOS version or the “Bottom”/“Top” tab layout: tap it and continue from step 3.
Using Chrome on iPhone? Tap the Share button in Chrome’s address bar, then Add to Home Screen. Or use Safari, as above.

Android · Chrome

Chrome may offer it by itself: if you see an Install app banner at the bottom, just tap it.
Samsung Internet: tap the menu ≡ → Add page to → Home screen.

Mac, Windows & Linux

Safari on Mac: open the app, then choose File → Add to Dock and click Add.
Firefox: can’t install web apps, but Finance works fine in a normal tab; bookmark it.

Then, inside the app

  1. Tap Start with starter categories (you can rename them any time).
  2. Optional: Settings → Currency to show amounts in taka, rupees, euros and more.
  3. Optional: Settings → Sign in with Google on each device to keep them in sync.
  4. Optional: add a free Finnhub key in Settings → Stock prices for live stock prices.

How your data is handled

On your device

Everything you enter is stored in your browser’s private storage on that device. The app works fully without signing in.

In your Google Drive (optional)

If you sign in with Google, Finance asks for one permission: to store its own data in a hidden app folder in your Drive. It cannot see, open or change any of your other files. Nobody else — including the developer — can read that folder.

Finance’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Read the full privacy policy and terms.
